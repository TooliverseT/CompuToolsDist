Aerospace Calculator – Orbital Mechanics, Kepler Orbit, Hohmann Transfer ΔV, ISA Atmosphere

Calculate orbital parameters from Keplerian or Cartesian inputs, estimate Hohmann transfer delta-v, and visualize 2D orbits. Includes ISA standard atmosphere (0-86 km) with temperature, pressure, density, speed of sound, Mach number, dynamic pressure, and drone thrust correction.

Aerospace Calculator Overview

This tool combines orbital mechanics and ISA atmosphere calculations in one interface for aerospace, robotics, and drone workflows.

  • Orbital tab: Keplerian/Cartesian input, orbital period, periapsis/apoapsis altitude, circular/escape speed, Hohmann transfer ΔV, 2D orbit view.
  • Atmosphere tab: ISA 0-86 km with temperature, pressure, density, speed of sound, Mach number, dynamic pressure, and thrust correction ratio.
  • Unit toggles: km/mi for orbital distances and °C/K/°F for atmosphere temperature output.
